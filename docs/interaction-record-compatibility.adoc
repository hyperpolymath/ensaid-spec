== Proposed ownership decisions

* eNSAID spec owns vocabulary and optional interchange contract.
* PanLL or another conforming eNSAID interface owns interaction presentation, not the interaction's universal identity or storage.
* BoJ owns its own capability, coordination and tool-execution boundaries. This profile may carry foreign keys to BoJ claims and receipts.
* Hypatia owns governance analysis, not general-purpose judgement or permission.
* Domain-specific validators own semantic evidence (for example, AffineScript compiler checks, proof ledger and capability matrix).
* K9/DEED policy owners govern permissions and compliance; the record preserves references rather than producing authorisation.
* Any storage or provenance substrate must establish identity and integrity separately. A predecessor link is not sufficient proof of diachronic identity.

== Acceptance conditions before ratification

. Specify a canonical identifier and attribution scheme; distinguish assertion from authenticated signature.
. Complete the reference-implementation audit (PanLL PCC Rust versus AffineScript ports).
. Prove that optional consumers can ignore this extension without breaking Sections 01–09.
. Decide privacy, sensitive reasoning and retention boundaries.
. Define failure and cancellation receipts, deduplication, and reconciliation for retried actions.
. Show conformance fixtures: missing attribution, no authority, unknown obligation, heuristic translation, missing receipt, and conflicting interpretations.
. Reconcile the initialisation marker and legacy A2ML conventions in separate scoped changes; do not hide them in this proposal.
